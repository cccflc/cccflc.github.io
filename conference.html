We are pleased to announce that the 2026 CCCFLC Conference will take place Saturday, October 17, 2026 (9:00 a.m. - 4:00 p.m.) at Chaffey College in Rancho Cucamonga.

Information about our annual conference (call for presentations, presentation application forms, applications to host the conference at your campus, etc.) is disseminated through our newsletter. If you're not receiving the newsletter in your email and would like to, please contact us at cccflc@gmail.com to let us know.

REGISTRATION

The conference registration includes breakfast and lunch. Parking is free.

STEP 1:

Click here, and then complete and submit the online pre-registration form.

STEP 2:>

Pay the registration.

  • Part-time Faculty: $45 pre-registration ($55 at the door)
  • Full-time Faculty: $55 pre-registration ($65 at the door
  • Other Attendees: $45 pre-registration ($55 at the door) 

PAYMENT OPTIONS:

These are listed on the pre-registration form as well.

Zelle - This is the safest and fastest way to send payment without additional fees being charged to the CCCFLC. Access via your bank's mobile app (it will show up as one of the transfer options). Some banks may also have Zelle available through their website. Send your payment to (310) 430-2006 or with the QR code below.

PayPal - Click here or use the QR code below. Otherwise, you can go to the PayPal app and send payment to cccflc@gmail.com. (The CCCFLC pays a transaction fee of 1.9% + $0.10.)

Venmo - Click here or use the QR code below. Otherwise, you can go to the Venmo app and send your payment to cccflc@gmail.com. (The CCCFLC pays a transaction fee of 1.9% + $0.10.)

Check - Make your check out to CCCFLC and send to 3401 McManus Ave., Culver City, CA 90232.

PAYMENT RECEIPT:

If you require a receipt of payment, please indicate so on the pre-registration form. Receipts will be emailed within 2 to 3 weeks after the conference. Thank you.
